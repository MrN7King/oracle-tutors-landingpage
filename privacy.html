🔒 Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to teach your child well

This page explains what we collect to match you with a tutor and run your lessons, why, and what control you have.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

Oracle Mentors ("we", "us") is an online tutoring service run by Oracle Mentors, located in Sri Lanka. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Privacy contact: privacy@oraclementors.com.

2. What we collect

  • Request form: your name, email, whether you're booking for yourself or your child, subject, and grade or level.
  • Messages: your name, phone number and message content when you contact us on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Lessons: schedule, time zone, learning goals, progress notes, and recordings if you ask for them.
  • Payments: payment confirmations and receipts. Payments are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Tutor applicants: CVs, qualifications, identity and reference details you send us when applying.

3. Children's data

Lessons for children must be booked by a parent or guardian, and we communicate with the parent or guardian, not the child. For a child we collect only a first name, age or grade, subject, learning goals and lesson notes.

Children should not send us personal data through the website. If you are under 18 (under 16 in EU countries), please ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If we learn that a child gave us data without parental consent, we will delete it. Lesson recordings of children are made or shared only on a parent or guardian's request, and are shared only with them.

4. Why we use your data and our legal basis

  • To respond to requests, match tutors, schedule and deliver lessons, and take payment. Basis: performing a contract or taking steps you ask for before one.
  • To keep records, prevent fraud and run a safe service. Basis: our legitimate interests, and legal obligations such as accounting.
  • To measure and improve our website. Basis: your consent, where required, otherwise legitimate interests.
  • To share progress notes and recordings, and to send optional offers. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that significantly affect you.

5. Who we share data with

  • Your matched tutor, who sees only what they need to teach (name, subject, level, goals, schedule). Tutors are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf: Google (Apps Script and Sheets for our form, Gmail, Analytics, Fonts), Meta (WhatsApp), PayPal, and Google Meet
  • Authorities or advisers where the law requires it or to protect safety or legal rights.

6. International transfers

We operate from Sri Lanka, and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. When we or our providers transfer data of people in the UK or EEA, we rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Unanswered enquiries: up to 6 months.
  • Student and lesson records: while you use our service, plus 12 months.
  • Lesson recordings: 30 days, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Payment and tax records: 6 years, as the law requires.
  • Unsuccessful tutor applications: up to 12 months.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR and similar laws, including Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data, or correct it
  • delete it or restrict how we use it
  • transfer it to you or another provider (portability)
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests, and withdraw any consent you gave.

Email us to use these rights. We reply within one month. Parents and guardians can exercise these rights for their child. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), your local EU data protection authority, or the relevant authority in your country. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

9. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It sets cookies and collects usage data. Where the law requires, we ask for your consent before loading it, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out add-on. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.

10. Security

We limit access to personal data to the people who need it, use trusted providers with encryption in transit, and review who has access regularly. No online service is completely secure. If a breach puts you at risk, we will notify you and the regulators as the law requires.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date above. For material changes we will tell you directly. Questions or requests: support@oraclementors.com.
