Questions

Good to know

How do you pick our tutor?

Every tutor on our roster passes an interview, a subject knowledge check, and a recorded demo lesson before they teach a real student. We match based on the subject, level, and goals you tell us — you don't need to browse or choose yourself.

What if the first lesson isn't the right fit?

Just let us know. We'll either refund your trial lesson or match you with a different tutor right away — no awkward conversations needed.

How do I pay?

We accept PayPal and card payments. You'll get a clear receipt after every payment, and you only pay for the sessions or packages you book — no hidden subscriptions.

What time zone will lessons be in?

Whatever works for you. Our tutors regularly teach early mornings, evenings, and weekends to fit schedules across the US, UK, Australia, and beyond.

What subjects do you actually cover?

If we have a tutor for it, we'll teach it — school subjects at every grade, test prep, university coursework, and instruments like piano, guitar, and violin. Not sure if we cover something? Just ask us directly.

Ready to meet your tutor?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you within a couple of hours.
