Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Our service

Oracle Mentors, run by Oracle Mentors, located in Sri Lanka ("we", "us"), matches students with vetted tutors for online one-to-one lessons. Tutors are independent professionals who teach through our platform and processes. We arrange, schedule and support lessons, and we are responsible for the matching and vetting service.

2. Who can book

You must be 18 or over to book. Parents and guardians book on behalf of children and are responsible for them in the lesson area, including supervision as appropriate for the child's age. By booking for a child, you confirm that you have authority to do so.

3. Matching and the trial lesson

After you send a request, we aim to propose a tutor within 48 hours, depending on subject and availability. A trial lesson lets you check the fit before committing to more sessions. We only charge half the session fee for the trial lesson. Submitting a request does not book or charge you.

4. First lesson guarantee

If the first lesson isn't the right fit, tell us within 48 hours and we'll match you with a different tutor. This guarantee covers one re-match per student.

5. Prices and payment

  • Prices on our site are in US dollars, with euro equivalents shown as estimates. Final prices are confirmed before you book and may vary by subject, level and tutor.
  • We charge 50% of the session fee up front to reserve your class, and the remaining 50% after the lesson is delivered.
  • We accept PayPal. You'll receive a receipt for each payment.
  • You pay only for sessions or packages you book. There is no subscription, and you can stop at any time.
  • Prices may include or exclude taxes depending on your location, any applicable tax is shown at booking.

6. Scheduling, cancellations and refunds

  • Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. With enough notice, we move the session or credit your deposit.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows may forfeit the up-front 50% as compensation for the tutor's reserved time.
  • If a tutor cancels or misses a session, we'll reschedule or fully refund you.
  • If a lesson is not delivered as described, contact us within 7 days and we will review it fairly.
  • Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights, including any right to cancel under the laws of your country.

7. Conduct and safeguarding

Everyone must behave respectfully. Tutors must teach professionally, keep communication to lesson matters through our approved channels, Parents must not share private information such as phone numbers, email addresses, or any other personal details with tutors. If parents choose to share this information, we cannot guarantee its security. This policy is strictly enforced to protect your privacy and safety. Tutors must never ask for or share inappropriate personal contact details. Tutors must not share their personal information with parents, doing so will result in immediate termination. Students and parents must not harass tutors or share lesson links publicly. If you have a safeguarding concern, email us immediately and we will act promptly. We may pause or end a lesson or account for serious breaches.

You may use them for personal study but may not copy, resell or publish them. Please don't record lessons yourself without telling us.
