🦉 About us

Tutoring that is affordable, personal and accountable

Oracle Mentors matches students anywhere in the world with vetted online tutors, so families get quality one-to-one teaching without the usual price tag.

Why we started

Good tutoring shouldn't depend on where you live or how much you can spend. We founded Oracle Mentors to make quality, affordable one-to-one tutoring more accessible to students worldwide, from primary school maths to university chemistry to a first guitar lesson.

We are a small team. When you message us, you speak to one of the two people who run the company.

How we work

  • We match, you don't browse. Tell us the subject, level and schedule, and we hand-pick a tutor from our roster.
  • Every tutor is screened. Candidates complete an interview, a subject knowledge check and a recorded demo lesson before they teach a real student.
  • You try before you commit. Start with a low cost trial. If the fit isn't right, we match you with someone new.
  • You stay informed. Parents and students get notes after each class, and recordings are available on request.

Our commitments
